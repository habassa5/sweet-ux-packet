SK-8 custom · SK-7 · VG-1

Camera: the iPhone's own selfie framing, and the still is the preview

"iOS default front-camera zoom for still and video unless SDK needs a small adjustment. VG ~1ft. Skin pre-capture image MUST match post-capture (SK-7). No extra zoom, no mirror flip. Only chrome/buttons change. VG and Skin must feel like one app; chrome may differ by function."SK-8

How I read it

  • "iOS default" is what the Camera app shows for a selfie at 1×: the front camera at its own zoom, the whole 3:4 frame fitted into the screen with dark bands above and below. Today Skin fills the screen by height and throws away 39 percent of the frame width; that crop is the "zoomed in" feeling (VG-1 report, section 6 of my catalog). It goes.
  • One nuance look must verify on the phone, not from prose: the Camera app's portrait selfie is itself a fixed crop of the sensor (Apple expands to the wider field only when you tap the expand control or rotate). AVFoundation's zoom 1.0 is the full sensor. So "default" means the Camera app's selfie, compared side by side on Salim's iPhone, and the crop factor that matches it is measured, not chosen. Vital Glimpse's ×1.2 may already be close to it.
  • SK-7 is absolute: preview, review and the kept still are the same frame at the same crop, mirrored the same way. A mirrored preview is every selfie a person knows; "no mirror flip" means nothing flips between preview and review. The still the provider analyses stays unflipped, as today.
  • "VG ~1ft" sizes the oval: a face about a foot away fills it, and the tip says so. Skin uses the same geometry. The two instruments differ only in chrome: Skin has a shutter and a review step; Vital Glimpse has the arc, the cue and no shutter.
  • "Unless SDK needs a small adjustment": if Perfect Corp reports faceTooSmall at that framing, the smallest crop that satisfies it is applied identically to preview, review and the sent still, and reported back with the number.

Look must

  1. PerfectCorpSkinCameraPreview: videoGravity resizeAspect, not resizeAspectFill. Stage background stays dark so the bands read as bands.
  2. No videoZoomFactor beyond the device default on either instrument. CaptureOvalGeometry holds one visible-frame fraction for both, measured against the Camera app on device; ×1.2 is the starting guess, not the answer.
  3. skinSendWindow stays unapplied: the kept still is the whole frame at the same crop as the preview (SK-7 already met at f8f2967; keep it met).
  4. Oval sized to a face at about a foot; VG tip "About a minute. Hold about a foot away."
  5. Report back: the measured crop, Perfect Corp's face-size verdict at that crop, and whether Shen's face-position cue is happy at a foot.
Agree to ship if look builds this, with one nod from Salim: that "iOS default" means the Camera app's selfie view at 1×, not the wider expanded selfie. The phones draw the Camera-app view.

AS-5 B+C · AS-6 · AS-7

Ask model: a new default, and a TestFlight-only Developer screen that really changes the Worker

"B+C: hide-until-sync AND secret TestFlight-only Developer Settings that MUST actually change the Ask worker. Enforce not in production. Research a standard TF-only gate. Slug/secret fields were empty — pick a working TF-only pattern and document it."AS-5

How I read it

  • Two deliverables. B: the Worker's default model moves off Grok 4.6 to the Claude model Salim names, at medium effort. C: a hidden Developer screen in TestFlight builds where he can switch model, reasoning and speed per device, and see the last answer's timing (that is also how AS-3 gets measured).
  • "Must actually change the Worker" means the request carries the override and the Worker honours it. Today the wire contract has no model field and the Worker pins the model from its environment. So: schema version 2 adds an optional developer block (model, effort, fast), the Worker validates it against an allowlist it owns, and only when the request also carries a developer key that exists solely in TestFlight archives. Production clients cannot send a valid override even if tampered with.
  • "From all available models" without slugs in the app: the Worker exposes its allowlist (slug, provider, which controls it supports), the screen fetches it. No slug is typed into the iOS source, ever.
  • The TestFlight-only gate, researched: an App Store and a TestFlight build are the same archive, so a compile flag cannot tell them apart. The standard runtime check is StoreKit 2's AppTransaction environment: sandbox for TestFlight, production for the App Store, xcode for local runs. The older receipt-name check (sandboxReceipt with no embedded provisioning profile) is the fallback for phones that cannot reach the App Store at that moment.
