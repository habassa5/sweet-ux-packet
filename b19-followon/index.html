3Who may actually switch the Ask worker

AS-5 · AS-6 · Fable question 3 · Astra “Authorization”

Salim's note: the Developer screen must actually change the Ask worker, enforced not in production, with a standard TestFlight-only gate. Both lanes gate the screen on StoreKit's AppTransaction environment (sandbox for TestFlight) and keep the route out of App Store builds. They differ on what authorizes the override once the screen is open. Fable: the request carries a developer key that exists only in TestFlight archives; the Worker validates the override against its own allowlist; anyone who knows the gesture on a TestFlight phone may switch that phone. Astra: a gesture or a client flag is not authorization; the Worker verifies an expiring, revocable per-tester beta capability server-side, shows the requested and the effective configuration separately, and only the acknowledged one is used.
