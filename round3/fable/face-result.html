Your usual, last 14 days: 34–42 ms. Today's dot sits above it.

What it is. HRV is how much the gap between your heartbeats changes. It rises and falls with rest, stress and illness, and it is personal: only your own usual is a fair comparison.

So what. A reading above your usual often follows good rest. Below it often follows a hard day, short sleep, or fighting something off. One check is one moment; the pattern over weeks is what matters.
