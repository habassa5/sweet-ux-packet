The licensed analysis returns fifteen concerns, zone scores for pores and wrinkles, a skin type with T-zone and U-zone, and a skin age, on a 1–100 scale where higher is better (vendor doc, RESEARCH.md §Skin). A person in a treatment room does not hear fifteen numbers; they hear what looks good, the two or three things worth attention, and when to see someone. That is the first screen. Every number is one tap away. Green / amber / red are Sweet-defined presentation bands on the vendor's own scale (proposal in RESEARCH.md), each always beside a word.
