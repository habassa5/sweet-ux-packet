## Cold walkthrough and measured evidence

Before: the opening headline describes a previous fix rather than the decision. A paragraph narrates packet history and asks the reviewer to pin and pick. The full-width who bar, navigation, grouping badges, lock banner, recommendations, and repeated section instructions all precede the artifacts. At 2560×1440 the first phone starts approximately 650px down and extends beyond the viewport. The sticky header measures 78.27px and footer 63.16px: approximately 141px of permanent obstruction. The Home decision follows both models plus a demos phone. The reviewer must remember an artifact while scrolling to its radio.

The source creates 62 iframe elements: 61 HTML mocks plus an unused zoom iframe. One additional phone is a PNG. Captions reach 78 words. Settings supplies Codex's left-entry Home but directs the reviewer back to Home to find its right-entry counterpart. Health and Ask share a vote despite being different surfaces; Ask is duplicated and an absent Fable mock consumes a full 874px placeholder.

After: at 2560×1440 all three opening Fable phones, captions, and the choice bar fit on screen. Phone tops align at approximately 339px and bottoms at 1213px. The first load creates seven nearby iframe elements. All 61 retained phone artifacts measure exactly 402×874. Captions are at most 25 words. The existing tag colors measure 6.36:1 (Fable/white) and 6.93:1 (Codex/white); they did not need recoloring.

## Verification and limits

- Rendered the published baseline, then the local implementation in Chromium at 2560×1440. Inspected the main Fable and Codex rows across the whole packet and the generated PDF; all 61 phone artifacts were loaded for print and checked at native geometry.
- Checked 1920, 1440, 1280, 900, 768, 430, and 390px widths. Phones retain 402×874 dimensions. No horizontal page overflow at widths ≥430px in the tested layouts. Under 430px, horizontal access is intentional to preserve a native phone.
- Exercised choices in both model rows, canonical value synchronization, keyboard arrow selection, direct comparison anchors and focus, reload restoration, separate Salim/Ranya drafts, initial v1 migration, legacy pins, independent Health/Ask choices, copy/download contents, and storage failure messaging.
- Intercepted FormSubmit requests and tested explicit success, ambiguous success payload, and HTTP failure. Checked canonical field names and notes, no `ui_` radio fields in the payload, and no browser script errors. No test answers were sent to the real endpoint.
- Generated a 62-page A4 PDF: 61 whole-phone pages plus the answer summary. Inspected Fable and Codex pages for top/bottom clipping and verified section identity and captions. Long notes can add summary pages; long content inside a mock remains the mock's own scroll, matching the on-screen artifact.
- The prepared Print packet / PDF action is the reliable path for a complete packet. Browser-native Ctrl/Cmd+P cannot await asynchronous loading of distant frames; the print stylesheet displays a warning if used before all phones are ready. This limitation is explicit rather than silently presenting blank artifacts as complete.
- Frame mounting is bounded by proximity for untouched screens, not by a fixed hard cap. A mock that the reviewer interacts with is retained to avoid destroying its state. Deliberately interacting with every mock can therefore retain many frames; printing intentionally loads all of them.
- Recovery checks: blocked a mock request, verified its named error, retried successfully, and confirmed a clicked mock survives scrolling away. Section jumps peaked at 17 mounted frames while retaining that interacted mock. Reviewer switching also retained both in-memory drafts with storage writes denied.
- Local Chromium checks do not establish Safari/Firefox equivalence or real FormSubmit delivery. Production publication is verified separately after pushing.

Verdict: the packet now supports a clear Fable-first, Codex-second visual decision. Full phone geometry, local captions, real Settings placement choices, FormSubmit, autosave, and both reviewers are preserved. Remaining limits follow native phone size, asynchronous browser printing, and the requirement to keep supporting evidence visible.
