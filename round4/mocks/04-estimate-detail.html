What it is

How much the gap between your heartbeats changes. Everyone's number is different, so yours is compared with your own.

Why people watch it

A higher resting heart rate variability is generally considered healthy: it means your heart adapts more easily to what your body needs. It tends to be lower after poor sleep, illness or a hard day. One reading means little; a pattern means more.

How to read yours

Look at where today sits inside your band, not at the number. Morning checks compare best with morning checks.

When to check in with a clinician

If you feel unwell, or a change persists for weeks, talk to a clinician. This estimate can't tell you why it changed.
