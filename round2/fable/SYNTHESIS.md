This is a choice with reasons, not an average. Where Salim already decided, the decision is applied and named. Where two mocks exist, one is recommended and the other is kept as the named alternative for him to pick "with the rest of the look".

Round 1 fixed how the app works: one instrument per capture, one camera screen per check, results that answer "how did I do?" before they list, History that is a history. Salim accepted most of it, chose Codex's Home rows and Gut layout, and asked for a second visual pass with more beauty, care and whimsy, still clean; meaning before numbers; eyes up at the camera; an Instagram X on capture; one quiet honesty line; no vendor names. ## The recommendation

1. **Home: direction A, candy cards, gear top-right.** Codex's IA to the letter (brand row, "A moment for you." with the spiral, three check rows in Codex's order and words, demos under one heading, three tabs). Each check is a card on the paper with a tinted icon tile and a small "go" coin at its trailing edge. That is how a row becomes a button without a chevron and without a full-width teal fill: the card lifts it, the tint names it, the coin says "go". The latest-check strip speaks only in baseline grammar. B (tiles as buttons) is warmer but needs a gradient surface DESIGN.md does not have; C (Lens band) is bold but heavier at the top than "clean, uncluttered" wants.

2. **Face capture: the oval, higher; coach on top; X that knows when to leave.** Centre at y = 345 so the eyes sit near the camera. Coach line above the oval, two lines at most, gone in two seconds, every line tied to a state the SDK reports. Blur outside the oval. Instagram X at top-left, hidden while the signal is good and back the instant the person needs it. Under the oval, a heart that beats with the SDK's heartbeat intervals and a thin drifting waveform: engaging, silent, no number. No disclaimer on capture. 3b (a live bpm beside the heart) is the named alternative; it is not recommended because a moving number gets watched and watching changes breathing.

3. **Face result: six tiles that say what a reading means.** "Within your usual", "Above your usual", "Lower than usual", built from the person's own last 14 days, with the number and the usual range as a caption in the tile's corner. First check: four tiles saying "Your starting point". HRV is rMSSD only, SDNN stays in the record. The tap sheet answers Ranya's "so what?" in two short paragraphs and shows the person's range as a shape. Risk models stay named as models, no vendor name, one row explaining why the missing ones are missing. One honesty line at the end. Salim's remaining pick: A's small captions or B's tap-to-see.

4. **Skin capture: one shape, one line.** Guide 85 % wide and high, "Fill the shape with your face." with three two-word reminders under it, Instagram X, live shutter, dim (not blur) outside because the whole still is what gets sent. Recommended and needing a product yes: Apple's on-device face rectangle to flip the stroke and the line ("Lovely. Hold still and tap."). Without it, the shape alone still teaches.

5. **Skin result: highlights and lowlights.** Skin type first, then Doing well → Worth attention → Needs care in the order a professional speaks, with a plain "why" from the vendor's zone scores. Numbers one tap away with the polarity spelled out and raw_score only. Green / amber / red are Sweet's presentation bands on the vendor's 1–100 and are always beside a word. "When to check in" names a dermatologist or a licensed esthetician and what to bring, never a treatment.

6. **Gut result: as Salim decided.** Codex layout, Fable tints on the surround, borderless, brown drawings untouched, "You" outlined, the hero's surround in the same band as the row.

7. **History: as Salim decided, plus meaning in every row.** No Samples; All / Face / Skin / Gut; the compare card at two checks; the compare screen in two columns with a word per pair and no arrows; at seven checks a trend with the person's usual as a band and a sentence that counts.

8. **Settings top-right, one sheet.** Apple Health, lb·in, reminders, account, data, about. Health tab keeps readings and connections only.

## The reasons, in one place

- **Meaning before numbers is a grammar, not a hiding rule.** Oura and WHOOP answer "so what?" with a word against the person's own baseline; the Gut result already does it with form; the packet's rows put the word before the number. Sweet uses one grammar everywhere: the sentence, then the shape, then the number on request. Nothing is withheld; the compare screen is the one place numbers lead because comparing is what numbers are for.
- **Personal usual, not population thresholds.** It is what the two wearables do, it matches the 2026 FDA wellness guidance (no clinical thresholds, no disease names), and it is the only comparison a one-minute camera estimate can honestly support.
- **Whimsy is a few deliberate things.** The spiral, the tinted icon tiles, the go coin, "Lovely.", the heart under the oval. Everything else is calm paper. That is how beauty and clean coexist.
- **Chrome earns its place or leaves.** The X hides during a good run; the coach fades; the disclaimer is one line at the end of a result and nowhere on capture; vendor names live in the record and on About.
- **Colour never carries meaning alone.** Every dot has a word, every band tile a number, "You" is a label. This keeps DESIGN.md §1.6 and §5 while giving Salim the green/amber/red he asked to consider.
