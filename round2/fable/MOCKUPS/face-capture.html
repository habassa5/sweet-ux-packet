The X: visible or hidden?

Salim offered two rules: always an Instagram X at top-left, or hide it until the person is out of position. The frames above use the second: X visible while positioning and whenever the signal drops; hidden while the measurement is running well; the whole screen still accepts a swipe-down to stop. A visible X during a good run is one more thing to look at, and the person's eyes should be on the camera. If Salim prefers always-visible, frame 3 gains the X of frame 1 and nothing else changes.
