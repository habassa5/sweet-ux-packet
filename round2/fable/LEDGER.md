## Cross-cutting

- **One quiet honesty line, not stacks.** Each result ends with one line ("Wellness estimates from a camera, kept on this iPhone. Not a diagnosis." / "A wellness look at one photo…" / the Gut line already shipped). Capture screens carry none. Tap sheets carry none; the screen beneath already does.
- **No vendor names in the UI.** "Risk models · from your readings and Apple Health", "the analysis's own scale". Vendor identity stays in the typed record and on the About page.
- **Meaning never colour-alone.** Every dot has its word; every band tile has its number and, when current, "You".
- **Whimsy without clutter.** The candy comes from the spiral, the tinted icon tiles, the go coin, one warm word on capture ("Lovely."), the heart under the oval. Nothing else decorates.
- **Not mocked this round** (see STATUS.md): Health tab body, Ask screen, Gut capture (round-1 Fable viewfinder stands), dark mode, XXL type, VoiceOver order.
